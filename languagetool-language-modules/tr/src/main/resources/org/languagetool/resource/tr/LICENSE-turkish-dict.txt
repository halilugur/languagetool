The Turkish POS tagger and synthesizer dictionaries (turkish.dict, turkish_synth.dict)
are derived from two sources:

1. Zemberek-generated inflected forms
   Source: https://github.com/ahmetaa/zemberek-nlp
   License: Apache License 2.0

2. UD Turkish-BOUN treebank (human-annotated gold data)
   Source: https://github.com/UniversalDependencies/UD_Turkish-BOUN
   License: Creative Commons Attribution-ShareAlike 4.0 (CC BY-SA 4.0)

The combined derivative is distributed under CC BY-SA 4.0 (the more restrictive of
the two compatible licenses).

The spelling dictionary (tr_TR.dict) additionally derives from the Hunspell tr_TR
dictionary by the Turkish Data Depository group
(https://github.com/tdd-ai/hunspell-tr), licensed MPL 2.0.
